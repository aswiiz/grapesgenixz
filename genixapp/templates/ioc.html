ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

What is Industry on Campus?

Industry on Campus is an innovative academic–industry collaboration designed to provide students with practical software engineering experience before graduation. The initiative enables colleges to establish an industry environment within the campus where students actively participate in live projects, enterprise software development, mobile application development, web engineering, and emerging technologies.

Through continuous mentorship from experienced software engineers, students develop technical expertise, teamwork, communication skills, and professional work ethics, making them industry-ready by the time they complete their academic programs.
