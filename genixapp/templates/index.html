ABOUT GRAPESGENIX

Get Trained by the Experts Who Do It Best

Founded in 2015, GrapesGenix Technical Solutions Pvt Ltd has emerged as one of Kerala's leading technology solution providers and IT training institutes.

We specialize in Enterprise Software Development, Web Engineering, Mobile Applications, Industry on Campus initiatives, Live Projects, Academic Training, Internships, and Career Development Programs.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between academic education and industrial engineering through practical software development, live projects, internships and expert mentorship.
